One component per alert, evaluated in this priority order; the highest active one shows for a configurable duration, three seconds by default. Each is a band with a colour, a label and a behaviour, in the same three formats as the flags. The only shapes are bands, outlined bands and two patterns; nothing else on the face is allowed to compete with them.

changeNotification · decreasing
Tracked values that change while the driver is in the car raise one notification at a time. Values that are zero or empty at start are ignored, and a change settles over a few frames before it shows, so that a rotary being turned through several positions raises one pop-up, not five. The trend arrow is drawn, and it wears text.secondary: which way a setting moved is information, not a state.

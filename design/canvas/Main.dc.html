openDash
Design system · tokens 0.3.0 · dash, second screens and plugin
The brand is achromatic. Colour is reserved for meaning.
Everything is layered. A colour starts as a step on a palette scale, becomes a meaning (danger, best, a surface, a text role) with a primary, a secondary and a tint shade, and is finally named for the one place it is used: the delta when slower, the last lap when it equals the session best, the blue flag. Nothing on any surface reads a palette step directly, and nothing on the dash face reads anything but a purpose token.
The parts are layered the same way. Elements (a label, a numeral, a segment) make components (a readout, a rev bar, one component per flag); components make cards, which are the changeable modules the plugin drops into slots; and a layout is a screen size that fixes the hero and declares how many slots it has. The dash face, the companion and the pit wall carry no identity colour at all, since SimHub renders all three; the plugin and the documentation are where openDash cyan lives.

05
Type · one family, two widths
Barlow Condensed · every numeral, on every surface
0123456789 1:42.318
Weight 600 to 700, tabular figures. SimHub exposes no OpenType features, so whether lap times tick without jitter depends on Barlow Condensed having tabular digits by default; the spike checks it, and the fallback is right-aligned values. Tracking −0.01em applies in the plugin and the documentation only, since SimHub renders everything else.
Barlow · labels, plugin copy, controls
Reinstall the dashboard into SimHub
Weight 400 for copy, 500 for labels and buttons, 600 for the rare heading. Labels are uppercase on every surface. On the dash face they carry no tracking, because SimHub text items have no letter-spacing property; in the plugin and in documentation they are tracked 0.14em; on the pit wall and the companion, which SimHub renders too, they are not.

07
Rules · the part that survives a redesign
1
Nominal is achromatic. A value at rest is white or grey. It takes a colour only when its meaning changes: faster, slower, caution, alarm, flag, session best. Colour is always an event, never decoration.
2
Three layers, always. Palette steps are never read directly. A surface reads semantic tokens; the dash face reads purpose tokens only. A new card names what it means, and the meaning already has a shade.
3
Cyan is identity, and it stays off the face. Wordmark, one primary action per panel, focus ring, links, progress. On the dash, the pit wall and the companion every chroma is a purpose token.
4
Numerals are always Barlow Condensed weight 600 or 700, tabular figures: lap times on the dash, table rows on the pit wall, version numbers in the plugin. Text that is not a number is Barlow.
5
One label idiom, two renderers. Barlow 500, uppercase, text.label, 4px above its value. 15px on the dash and 13px on the second screens with no tracking, since SimHub has none; 12px tracked 0.14em in the plugin.
6
Hierarchy comes from size, not weight or colour. The dash runs 17:1 from gear to label. The plugin runs just over 2:1 from wordmark to label and never adds bold to make something important.
7
Hard edges. Radius 0 on the dash, with the shift segment as the one exception at 2. Radius 2 also on plugin controls and on the segment mark beside the wordmark, and nowhere else. A status dot is a 6px square.
8
Zones are separated by 1px rules, never boxes. Slots are separated by rules; the only filled rectangle is a list row, an input or a pop-up, in surface.zone. Nothing floats, nothing casts a shadow.
9
The hero never moves; everything else is a card. Gear, speed, rev bar, flag and pit limiter are fixed per layout. Every other value is a card that fits every slot of its layout, chosen from the plugin. A card that needs more room is two cards.
10
One component per flag and per alert. Twenty-five alerts share one band anatomy, solid, outlined or patterned, and differ in colour, label, priority and duration. One shows at a time; none is drawn when none is active; a round face uses its ring instead of a band.
11
Motion only carries meaning. The rev bar flashes at 8 Hz on redline, a flag band at 2 Hz, a pop-up lasts 3 s. The plugin animates one thing, a progress bar. Nothing eases in, nothing bounces.
12
Icons are drawn, not typed. 16px in controls, 20px alone, 1.5px stroke, square caps and mitre joins, one colour from the text scale. Never emoji, never a filled glyph next to a stroked one.
08
What changed in tokens 0.3.0, and what is still open
tokens.json moved to 0.3.0 with this canvas, and every resolved value that existed before is unchanged. What is new, for review: the palette layer with 100 and 300 steps (new hex values, off the face); the semantic layer with primary, secondary and tint per meaning; the purpose layer; size.gearSm 180; the ui ramp with numeralLg 24; control tokens; card rungs and padding; indicator sizes for the flag band, the flag ring, the pit limiter and the pop-up; the screen list with a slot count and grid per size, including the companion and pit wall sizes; and, with the alert catalogue, purpose.flag.red, orange and debris, purpose.alert for penalties, incidents, power warnings and push to pass, purpose.trend, purpose.class for the chips, and durations for alerts, notifications and the lap review.
Still open: the tyre temperature thresholds behind purpose.tyre.cold and hot, left as [T_COLD] and [T_HOT] until validated in iRacing; whether Barlow Condensed digits are tabular, which the spike checks; and the exact SimHub style values (SHButtonPrimary, SHToggleButton, section titles) that the plugin panel inherits, which are lifted from SimHub's resource dictionaries at build time rather than drawn here.

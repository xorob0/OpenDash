Hero and cards
Dash face, 1:1. Four layers: element, component, card, layout. Labels carry no tracking here, since SimHub text items have none.
layers
Every part belongs to one layer and is built only from the layer below.
Element
label · numeral at a rung · unit · denominator · rule · segment · band · dot · bar
then
Component
readout · grid · revBar · revArc · one component per flag · pitLimiter · popUp · pageIndicator · listRow · trackMap · radar · trace
then
Card
a changeable module built from components, one card per value the driver may want: delta, position, fuel, relative ahead, track map
then
Layout
a screen size: hero module plus N equal slots, each slot taking any card from the plugin
elements
The atoms. Nothing here carries meaning on its own; a colour arrives with the purpose token of the component that uses it.

flag.chequer · component ChequeredFlag · pattern, no label
Six flag components share one anatomy, the band, and differ in colour, label and behaviour. One flag shows at a time, in the scope priority order black, chequered, yellow, blue, white, green. When no flag is out nothing is drawn: the band is absent, not grey. On a round face the outer ring takes the colour instead of a band.

A card picks its rung from the slot it lands in: L from 250 px wide and 150 px tall, M from 180 px wide, S below. Every card fits every slot of its layout, so any card can be assigned to any slot from the plugin. A card that needs more room is two cards, which is why the relative view is one card for the cars ahead and one for the cars behind.

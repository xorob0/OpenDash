what needs what
Relative, leaderboard, lap history, sectors, brakes, inputs, car settings, DRS, weather and clock read SimHub properties that exist today. Track map and radar are SimHub items of their own, GeneratedMapItem and RadarItem, which the generator does not yet model. Fuel calculator, stint and tyre wear projections need computed telemetry, which is the point at which the plugin earns a DataUpdate. Nothing here changes the MVP: the slot mechanism is what makes them cheap later.

Zones and their catalogues
A face is not a grid of cards. It is a rev bar, a bar of settled values, three zones across the body and a band, and each zone cycles through its own catalogue from a wheel button. The plugin decides which pages are enabled per zone, which one opens, and which one a quick glance shows.
anatomy
The same five parts on every rectangular face. Only their sizes change.
Rev bar
shift lights in their well, full width, never moves
Top bar
what the car is set to and where the session is: three fields a driver may swap, and a settings strip that hides what the game does not expose
Zone B
a page from the catalogue
Zone A
gear, gear and speed, speed, or the track
Zone C
a page from the catalogue
Band D
fuel by default, and six more pages that suit a wide short band
The flag takes the band over while one is out
an alert outranks fuel, so the two share the band rather than each keeping its own strip
the bar
Settled values, which a driver reads between corners rather than at speed. Each end is a field with its own small catalogue; the centre strip carries the car settings and hides any the game does not expose.

zones B and C · twenty-one pages, four shapes each
A zone has a shape, not just a width, and every page answers to it. A long shallow zone takes one rank; a tall narrow one stacks its values and sizes them to the height; the two in between take ranks. Nothing is scaled down: a page drops its secondary rows and keeps the reading it exists for.
